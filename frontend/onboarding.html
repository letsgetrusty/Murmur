You're all set

Here's how to use Murmur. It lives in your menu bar.

Fn Hold to dictate — release to paste at your cursor.
Fn+Ctrl Hold to dictate and clean it up with the LLM.
⌘+⇧+R Read the selected text (or clipboard) aloud.

No Fn key on your keyboard? Pick a different dictate key from the menu-bar icon → Settings. Change any of this there later, too.
